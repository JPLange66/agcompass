Operating board

Arizona agriculture is almost entirely irrigated. The job of this board is to put market price, water cost, allocation, diesel, and crop choice on one screen so a grower can see the most profitable path that actually fits the water he is allowed to use.

The cab screen above is NOAA GOES-18 Pacific Southwest GeoColor (NESDIS public rolling latest, 1200×1200). It updates when you reload. If it fails, the page tries the GOES-19 Southwest image, which also resolved on Nov 10, 2026, Arizona time. It is a regional satellite picture, not a field-level forecast.

Irrigation district, crop plan, and acre-foot calculator

Selecting a district loads its published 2024 assessment and included allotment. Both are editable. The allocation caps the crop plan. Demo defaults to a Yuma lettuce / carrot / tomato mix. Add alfalfa, cotton, feed corn, or sorghum to compare water-intensive field crops against higher-value produce.

Tomato price is $0. No Western Arizona tomato FOB was on the Nov 9 shipping-point report, so tomato revenue is zero until you enter a contract or shed price.

What the defaults actually are

Input lines and sponsor slots

Each line has a supplier field and an editable quoted price so a live rep quote can sit next to the public feed. Sponsorships are inventory slots only — no company has signed. Default is that these line totals are not added into profit, because UA field-crop operating costs already include fertilizer, seed, chemicals, and machinery. Quantities start at 0 for that reason.

Opportunity cards

Price context, import share, season, and profit per acre-foot from the current plan. Cards are decision prompts, not planting recommendations.
